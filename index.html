    Why BBS exists

    Students can spend years preparing for examinations, yet receive little practical preparation for employment, entrepreneurship, professional relationships, and life after graduation.

    BBS is a student development and opportunity-oriented community that connects academic learning with practical growth. Read our story.

    What we focus on

      Why join the community

      • Meet students from different courses and universities.
      • Learn practical skills alongside your studies.
      • Hear about scholarships, internships, and programmes offered by others.
      • Collaborate with peers and explore business and leadership.

      Your growth begins with the connections you build.
